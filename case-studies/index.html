Case Studies

Four problems. Four measurable outcomes.

Each of these started with a business problem that cost money, not a wish for better satisfaction scores. The method is the same throughout — start with customers, then prove it with numbers.

oobainsure · 2025 – 2026

Building a brand position from 17,930 customer voices

Four insurance product lines, no consistent message, and low cross-sell. I analysed 17,930 customer responses to find the real behavioural barriers, then built a brand position from what customers actually said.

"Protection you understand" — wills identified and validated as the gateway product oobainsure · Jan 2025 – present

From 5.2 to 9.9 — rebuilding customer trust in public

oobainsure's public reputation looked far worse than its actual service. I built two triggers to capture good experiences at the moments customers were most satisfied, and let the real experience balance the public record.

Trust Index 5.2 → 9.9 · NPS -5 → +66 · category rank #39 → #7 ooba Home Loans · May – Dec 2024

Five times the conversion rate, proven in parallel

Standard prequalification converted at 0.41%. I designed a purchase-intent model and ran it alongside the existing process for eight months so the result would be credible, not claimed.

2.13% vs 0.41% conversion · R77M in registered home loans oobainsure · Aug 2025 – Jul 2026

From zero digital presence to 674 insurance leads in 12 months

oobainsure had no way for a customer to find it independently online. I built the acquisition channel and the customer lifecycle programme together, from nothing, on a R37,600 budget.

1.36M organic impressions · 674 leads · 32.2% lifecycle engagement
