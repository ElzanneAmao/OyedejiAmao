Case Study — Brand Positioning

Building a brand position from 17,930 customer voices

Clientoobainsure (part of the ooba Home Loans Group)
RoleGroup Marketing Manager & Customer Experience Specialist
Period2025 – 2026
01The Problem

oobainsure had four insurance product lines but no clear brand positioning. The marketing team was communicating differently about each product, and there was no consistent message about what oobainsure stood for. Customers did not understand the full range of what was available to them, and cross-sell between products was low.

02What I Did

I started with the customers. I collected and analysed 17,930 customer responses to understand how policyholders actually thought about insurance, what language they used, what they feared, and what made them disengage. Four behavioural barriers kept coming up: customers did not understand what they were covered for, they found insurance language confusing, they did not trust that claims would be paid, and they saw insurance as something they had to have rather than something that helped them.

From those findings, I developed the brand positioning: "Protection you understand." Simple, direct, and built on what customers actually said they needed — clarity, not complexity.

I then mapped oobainsure's four product lines to the homebuyer lifecycle. A first-time buyer has different insurance needs from someone who has owned a home for ten years. By aligning the products to where customers were in their journey, we could offer the right product at the right time. This work validated that wills were the gateway product — customers who started with a will converted to core insurance products at 4%, with a 3–4% increase in leads for other product lines.

03The Result

A single brand position grounded in customer research, not internal assumptions. Four product lines mapped to lifecycle stages with a clear cross-sell path. A gateway product identified and validated with conversion data.

The positioning work fed into the broader ooba group rebrand. Within oobainsure, it changed everything customers see and hear: website copy, sales collateral, campaign messaging, the tagline, and how every message is written on social media. The research output did not sit in a presentation — it became the foundation for how the business communicates with customers across every channel.
