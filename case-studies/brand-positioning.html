Case Study 3 · Brand Positioning

Building a brand position from 17,930 customer voices

Clientoobainsure
RoleGroup Marketing Manager & Customer Experience Specialist
Period2025 – 2026
17,930 customer responses analysed
4 behavioural barriers identified
2 independent studies confirming the findings
4% conversion on wills, the validated gateway product

This case study sits in a different part of my career. A group restructure brought marketing and customer experience together under one role, which I took on. The work here is CX capability applied to a marketing problem: using customer insight to build something the business didn't have.

01

The Problem

oobainsure had four insurance product lines but no clear brand positioning. Marketing was communicating differently about each product, there was no consistent message about what oobainsure stood for, and cross-sell between products was low. Customers didn't understand the full range of what was available to them.

02

What I Did

I analysed 17,930 customer responses and found four behavioural barriers: customers didn't understand their cover, found the language confusing, didn't trust that claims would be paid, and saw insurance as an obligation rather than something that helped them. The FSCA's 2023 national research and Vox Research both validated the same findings independently.

From there, I built the brand positioning: “Protection you understand.” I mapped oobainsure's four product lines to homebuyer lifecycle stages, which gave the sales team a clearer conversation and validated wills as the gateway product at 4% conversion.

03

The Result

The positioning anchored all communication across social media, sales collateral, campaign messaging, and the website refresh. For the first time, oobainsure had a consistent message across every channel and a clear reason for customers to choose it.

4%conversion on wills, validated as the gateway product
4product lines mapped to homebuyer lifecycle stages

Measurement of the positioning's impact on conversion and cross-sell is underway.

04

What This Changed

The positioning came from what customers said. That's the CX discipline applied to marketing: let the evidence set the direction. Whether it moves the commercial numbers is still being measured, and I'll update this case study when that data is available.
