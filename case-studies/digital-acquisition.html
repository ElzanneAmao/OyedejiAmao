Case Study 4 · Digital Acquisition

From zero digital presence to a continuous insurance pipeline

Clientoobainsure
RoleGroup Marketing Manager & Customer Experience Specialist
PeriodNovember 2025 – August 2026
974 insurance leads across five product lines
1.4M organic search impressions
R6.45 cost per conversion
32.2% lifecycle engagement vs a 20–25% benchmark

Like the brand positioning work, this sits in my current marketing and CX role. oobainsure had no digital acquisition capability at all, so I built it, applying customer understanding to a channel the business hadn't addressed on a limited budget.

01

The Problem

All insurance leads came through the home loan referral path. There was no way for a customer to find oobainsure independently online, compare products, or request a quote digitally. The business had a single source of leads and no fallback.

02

What I Did

The brand positioning gave me a clear message. The next step was building the channel to deliver it. I redesigned the website with SEO and AI-search optimisation, then launched paid search campaigns across three product lines on a total budget of approximately R37,600.

At the same time, I built oobainsure's first customer lifecycle programme. Policyholders and lapsed leads were segmented into three cohorts: existing customers, new decliners, and historical decliners. Each cohort entered a nurture sequence designed to re-engage, build trust, and convert.

03

The Result

1.4Morganic search impressions
974insurance leads across buildings, bond protection, home contents, vehicle and wills
38.4%brand search click-through rate
R6.45cost per conversion
660policyholders reached by the lifecycle programme each month
32.2%lifecycle engagement, against a 20–25% financial services benchmark
04

What This Changed

oobainsure went from having no way for customers to find it online to a pipeline that generates leads independently of the home loan referral path. The digital channel brings new customers in and the lifecycle programme keeps existing ones engaged. Both were built from nothing, on a small budget, without a dedicated team. Downstream conversion data from the lifecycle programme is expected later in 2026, and I'll update this case study when it's available.
