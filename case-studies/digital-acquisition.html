Case Study — Digital Acquisition

From zero digital presence to 674 insurance leads in 12 months

Clientoobainsure
RoleGroup Marketing Manager & Customer Experience Specialist
PeriodAugust 2025 – July 2026
01The Problem

oobainsure had no digital acquisition channel. All insurance leads came through the home loan referral path — when a customer applied for a bond through ooba, they were offered insurance. There was no way for a customer to find oobainsure independently online, compare products, or request a quote digitally.

02What I Did

I built the channel from scratch. The first step was a full website redesign with SEO and AI-search optimisation so that oobainsure would appear when customers searched for home insurance, building insurance, or bond protection. I then launched paid search campaigns across three product lines with a total budget of approximately R37,600.

At the same time, I built oobainsure's first customer lifecycle programme. Existing policyholders and lapsed leads were segmented into three cohorts — existing customers, new decliners (people who recently chose not to take a policy), and historical decliners (people who declined in the past). Each cohort entered a four-stage nurture sequence: re-engage, show value, build trust, convert. The programme was designed to bring lapsed leads back into the funnel and to cross-sell additional products to existing policyholders at the right moment in their homeownership journey.

04Why It Matters

This case study shows two things working together. The digital channel brings new customers in. The lifecycle programme keeps existing customers engaged and brings lapsed ones back. Both were built from nothing within a single year, on a small budget, by connecting customer insight to marketing execution.
