Case Study 1 · Expert Verification

Five times the conversion rate from better-qualified leads

Clientooba Home Loans
RoleGroup Head of Customer Experience
PeriodMay – December 2024
5× the conversion rate of the standard process
R77M in registered bonds
246 → 47 leads needed per registered bond
8 months run in parallel against the old model
01

The Problem

ooba's standard prequalification process sent a large volume of leads into the sales funnel, but the conversion rate from lead to registered home loan was 0.41%. The contact centre was spending time on leads that were unlikely to convert, and the high volume made it hard to focus on the ones that would.

02

What I Did

I designed a lead qualification model called Expert Verification. Instead of sending every lead straight to a bond consultant, the model filtered leads through a purchase-intent assessment: how soon does this person intend to buy? Only leads with near-term intent were routed to consultants immediately. The rest entered a nurture path until they were ready.

I didn't replace the existing process. I ran both side by side for eight months, with leads allocated to each under the same market conditions. The business could evaluate the new model against the standard one in its own data, and the teams working the leads adapted as the evidence came in.

03

The Result

Expert Verification compared with the standard prequalification process over eight months
Measure Expert Verification Standard process
Leads2,58816,959

The new model produced 80% as many registered bonds from 15% of the leads. Leads needed per registered bond dropped from 246 to 47.

04

What This Changed

The parallel test meant the result couldn't be explained by market timing or lead quality differences. It showed that a smaller pool of better-qualified leads converted at five times the rate, and the contact centre could focus its time where it mattered. The R77M in registered home loans came from a fraction of the volume the standard process required.
