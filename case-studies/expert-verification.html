Case Study — Lead Qualification

Five times the conversion rate, proven in parallel

Clientooba Home Loans
RoleGroup Head of Customer Experience
PeriodMay – December 2024
01The Problem

ooba's standard prequalification process sent a large volume of leads into the sales funnel. But the conversion rate from lead to registered home loan was low — 0.41%. The contact centre was spending time on leads that were unlikely to convert, and the high volume made it hard to focus on the ones that would.

02What I Did

I designed a new lead qualification model called Expert Verification. Instead of sending every lead straight to a bond consultant, the model filtered leads through a purchase-intent assessment. Leads were scored by how soon they intended to buy: 0–3 months, 3–6 months, or 6+ months. Only leads with near-term purchase intent were routed to consultants immediately. The rest entered a nurture path until they were ready.

To prove the model worked, I did not replace the existing process. I ran both in parallel for eight months, with leads allocated to each. Same period, same market conditions, comparable lead pools. The results spoke for themselves.

03The Results
Expert Verification

2.13% conversion

2,588 leads produced 55 registered home loans.

Standard process

0.41% conversion

16,959 leads produced 69 registered home loans.

The new model converted at five times the rate. It produced 80% as many registered bonds from 15% of the leads. The average bond size was R1.4 million, and the model delivered R77 million in registered home loans over the eight-month period.

04Why It Matters

The parallel test is what makes this result credible. It was not a before-and-after comparison where market conditions could explain the difference. Both models ran at the same time. The only variable was the qualification method.

For the business, this meant fewer leads needed per registered bond — from 246 down to 47. That is a direct reduction in cost per acquisition and a better use of every consultant's time.
