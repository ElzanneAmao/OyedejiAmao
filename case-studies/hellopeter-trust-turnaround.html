Case Study 2 · Hellopeter Trust Turnaround

From 5.2 to 9.9: rebuilding customer trust in public

Clientoobainsure
RoleGroup Head of CX, then Group Marketing Manager & CX Specialist
PeriodJanuary 2025 – present
5.2 → 9.9 Hellopeter Trust Index
2.0 → 4.64 star rating
−50 → +53 Net Promoter Score
#39 → #6 home insurance category ranking
01

The Problem

oobainsure's Hellopeter Trust Index sat at 5.2 out of 10. Its star rating was 2.0 and it ranked #39 in the home insurance category. For a business that depends on referrals from the home loan side of the group, a poor public reputation was working against conversion. Customers referred to oobainsure for insurance could see the reviews before they decided whether to take the policy.

02

What I Did

When I looked at the data, oobainsure was not delivering a bad experience. Good interactions were happening daily across sales and claims. The problem was that none of them were showing up publicly. Hellopeter was the only source of truth customers had, and the only people going there unprompted were the unhappy ones.

I built two automated triggers, one after positive sales calls and one after successful claims through fulfilment partners, that sent customers a WhatsApp message with a direct link to rate oobainsure on Hellopeter. No extra steps, no friction. The request arrived at the moment the customer felt good about the interaction. The negative reviews were real, but the public picture was one-sided because only dissatisfied customers were motivated to leave feedback.

03

The Result

oobainsure's public reputation measures in January 2025 compared with August 2026
Measure January 2025 August 2026
Hellopeter Trust Index5.29.9
Star rating2.04.64
Net Promoter Score−50+53
Home insurance category ranking#39#6

All publicly verifiable on Hellopeter.

04

What This Changed

When referred customers research oobainsure before deciding, what they find now reflects the actual experience across the full customer base. The programme didn't suppress negative reviews: it surfaced the positive experiences that were already happening but weren't visible. That shift in public perception supports the referral path that the business depends on.
