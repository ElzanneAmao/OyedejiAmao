Case Study — Trust Turnaround

From 5.2 to 9.9 — rebuilding customer trust in public

Clientoobainsure
RoleGroup Head of CX, then Group Marketing Manager & CX Specialist
PeriodJanuary 2025 – present
01The Problem

oobainsure's Hellopeter Trust Index sat at 5.2 out of 10. Its star rating was 2.0. In the home insurance category, it ranked #39. For a company that depends on referrals from the home loan side of the business, a poor public reputation was actively working against conversion. Customers who were referred to oobainsure for insurance could see the reviews before they decided whether to take the policy.

02What I Did

The first thing I needed to understand was why the public picture looked so bad. When I looked at the data, oobainsure was not delivering a terrible experience across the board. Good interactions were happening every day — in the sales call centre, through the claims process, at renewal. The problem was that none of those good experiences were showing up publicly. Hellopeter was the only source of truth customers had, and the only people going there unprompted were the unhappy ones. The positive experiences were real, but invisible.

The fix was simple in concept: make sure the good experiences get captured in the same place as the bad ones. I built two triggers:

Trigger 1

After a good sales interaction

When a customer had a positive call with the sales centre, the system sent a WhatsApp message with a direct link to rate oobainsure on Hellopeter. No friction, no extra steps. The request came at exactly the moment the customer felt good about the interaction.

Trigger 2

After a successful claim

oobainsure uses external fulfilment partners to process claims. When a claim was resolved and the customer gave positive feedback, the fulfilment partner triggered the same WhatsApp message, taking the customer straight to Hellopeter.

This was not about hiding bad reviews or gaming the system. The negative reviews were legitimate and the underlying problems still needed fixing. But the public picture was distorted because only unhappy customers were motivated to leave a review. By capturing feedback at the two moments where satisfaction was highest — a good sales call and a resolved claim — the Hellopeter profile started reflecting what was actually happening across the full customer base, not just the complaints.

03The Result
5.2 → 9.9
Hellopeter Trust Index
2.0 → 4.6
Star rating
-5 → +66
NPS
#39 → #7
Home insurance category ranking

These numbers are publicly verifiable on Hellopeter — anyone can check them.

04Why It Matters

This was not a marketing campaign. It was a CX programme that changed a commercial outcome. A higher trust score and better ranking mean more referred customers convert to policyholders. The mechanism was straightforward: identify the moments customers are satisfied, make it easy for them to say so publicly, and let the real experience balance out the public record. The negative reviews did not disappear — the positive ones just started showing up too.
