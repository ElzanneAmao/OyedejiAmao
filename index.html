Customer Experience & CX Strategy — Cape Town, South Africa

I make customer experience work for the business, not just the customer.

Twenty years in customer experience. The last decade in South African banking, insurance and home finance. Head of Customer Experience at both Absa and the ooba Home Loans group.

I fix three problems that cost companies money: customer journeys that lose people before they convert, onboarding that fails to activate new customers, and organisations that collect data about their customers but never act on it. I have built CX functions from scratch, designed processes that changed how businesses operate, and connected customer insight to commercial results you can measure.
